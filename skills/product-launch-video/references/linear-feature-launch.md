# Linear Feature Launch Reference

Use this reference for the `feature-launch@1.x` template. "Linear" describes editorial restraint and pacing, not Linear's brand assets.

## Narrative shape

1. Result: show the finished or changed product state immediately.
2. Mechanism: reveal the smallest interaction or workflow detail that explains the result.
3. Proof: dwell on the verified UI state, output, or checkpoint.
4. Close: restate the release value using product evidence, not spectacle.

For three beats, combine mechanism and proof. For five beats, split one complex capability into two evidence-backed states without inventing a new claim.

## Visual grammar

- Treat product Evidence as the hero image.
- Prefer a stable full frame, framed detail, or evidence-with-copy split chosen from the allowlist.
- Use decisive cuts softened by the approved continuous transition preset.
- Use focus moves sparingly. Crop only when the proof remains identifiable and legible.
- Keep backgrounds flat and tonal. Do not use gradient text, decorative blobs, glass cards, generic cinematic effects, or recreated product UI.
- Preserve readable product UI in both compiler variants; let the compiler adapt composition geometry.

## Rhythm

- Enter copy after the product state is visible or concurrently with its reveal.
- Align a state change or proof badge to the beat's strongest motion point.
- Hold the hero frame long enough to inspect the evidence.
- Do not animate elements out before the transition. Let the transition carry the scene change.
