# Built-in SFX sources and license

PurpleInk bundles 20 small sound-effect presets as frozen local files. All source assets are dedicated to the public domain under Creative Commons CC0. Attribution is not required; these notes preserve provenance for maintainers and redistributors.

## Kenney

- UI Audio: <https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio>
- Interface Sounds: <https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds>
- Impact Sounds: <https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds>
- Digital Audio: <https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio>
- Kenney license guidance: <https://kenney.nl/support>

Kenney identifies every pack above as Creative Commons CC0 and permits commercial use. The bundled files were transcoded to 48 kHz stereo Ogg Vorbis and renamed to stable PurpleInk preset IDs.

## OpenGameArt

- Swishes Sound Pack by artisticdude: <https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack>
- Keyboard Soundpack #1 by unicaegames: <https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes>

Both source pages identify their downloads as Creative Commons CC0. PurpleInk uses three short swishes and a 1.5-second excerpt of the generated fast typing recording, transcoded to 48 kHz stereo Ogg Vorbis.
